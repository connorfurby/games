## Thesis

Each era multiplies income ~1,000×, and the store you started with stays the engine.
Every purchase visibly changes the store. Every upgrade/perk is worth ≥ ×1.25 on something, or a new verb.

## Stat engine

Everything you own multiplies named stats (`content/upgrades/stats.ts`). Same-stat bonuses add;
stats multiply in the formulas; each stat is soft-capped as (1 + bonus)^0.7. Ranks 10 and 25 double
a node; every 50 ranks in a tree adds 10% mastery. Sources: upgrades, doctrines, Empire rank rewards,
finished Projects, Goodwill. The Empire screen lists the resulting multipliers.

## Rival chains

One named rival opens per era from Era 2: Penny Pantry (Dot Mercer), FreshWay Markets (Ines Okafor),
MegaMart Global (Conrad Vale), Halo Provisions (Sera Lindqvist). An independent rival opens a store every
few minutes; each store cuts demand (`× 1 / (1 + stores × 0.006)`). A **price war** costs 5% of the
buyout, closes two stores and freezes growth for 10 minutes. A **buyout** costs more per store, folds
the stores into the network at 0.25 flagships each, and grants Empire XP. Buying every open rival also
stops the random rival events. Two new stats feed it: `rivalCut` and `dealCut` (Brand and Capital trees).

## Empire Map

Empire › Map draws everything owned as rings around the flagship: City (chain stores, Penny),
Region (districts, cold plants, FreshWay), World (lanes, franchises, MegaMart), Orbit (barges, Halo,
biomass cleared). Zoom tabs open by era; tap a pin for details. Worlds add System (era 7), Cluster
route arcs (era 8), and Galaxy embassy pins (era 11).

## Worlds (Eras 7–12)

Horizon panels share a Fleet-style shell: one-sentence purpose → goal strip (progress, ETA, layer
payoff) → outcome scene → single primary action → legible puzzle work → meters/staff. File the
standard stays visible once; puzzle currencies gate verbs; cash buys buildings. Convoy packing uses
five bays `[2,2,3,3,4]` and eight crates; score = cargoFrac × routeMatch. Signal shows world/modal
clues and an 8-line agent log. Last Aisle wires `fillLastLine` with Accept/Reject stakes before 0.8.

## Economy model (2.0)

income ≈ **flagship sales × value ladder × network**

- **Flagship** — the store you actually run (shelf, unpack, checkout, price). Unchanged mini-game.
- **Value ladder** (`VALUE` in balance.ts) — storefront tier × era. Premium stock: price *and* wholesale cost scale, so profit scales and best price stays put.
- **Network** (`sim/systems/network.ts`) — chain stores, franchises, districts, cold Momentum, ocean lanes, barges. Each copies flagship sales at `NETWORK.margin`. Every repeatable has geometric cost growth so each layer adds roughly ×3–×6 per era.
- **Word of mouth** — demand × (1 + 0.45·log10(1 + baskets/60)).
- **Goals** (`content/goals.ts`) — the campaign spine. Drives the HUD Next-goal bar and the pacing bot.

## Bugs found while rebalancing (fixed)

- Cold plants were deleted (not shed) on brownout, and a single plant always browned out — the first $25k plant vanished. Now shed plants wait offline and return; one plant covers its own demand.
- Auto-restock compared absolute unit cost to a $0.85 cap.
- Control accrued as 0.002% of cash per second, making lanes free for a rich player. Now log-scaled.
- Barges and franchises had flat prices (runaway). Now geometric.
- Chain Attention drain ran after the refill, so a full bar was impossible with stores; cap-sized memos were unbuyable. Tourney While Full also ate the bar a desk memo was waiting for.
- Reach-in memo only appeared after produce spoiled, which could stall the cold path for tidy players.
- Per-tick sale carry was capped at 2 units regardless of tick size.
