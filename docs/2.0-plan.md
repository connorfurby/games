## Thesis

Each era multiplies income ~1,000×, and the store you started with stays the engine.
Every purchase visibly changes the store. Every upgrade/perk is worth ≥ ×1.25 on something, or a new verb.

## Economy model (2.0)

income ≈ **flagship sales × value ladder × network**

- **Flagship** — the store you actually run (shelf, unpack, checkout, price). Unchanged mini-game.
- **Value ladder** (`VALUE` in balance.ts) — storefront tier × era. Premium stock: price *and* wholesale cost scale, so profit scales and best price stays put.
- **Network** (`sim/systems/network.ts`) — chain stores, franchises, districts, cold Momentum, ocean lanes, barges. Each copies flagship sales at `NETWORK.margin`. Every repeatable has geometric cost growth so each layer adds roughly ×3–×6 per era.
- **Word of mouth** — demand × (1 + 0.45·log10(1 + baskets/60)).
- **Goals** (`content/goals.ts`) — the campaign spine. Drives the HUD Next-goal bar and the pacing bot.

## Bugs found while rebalancing (fixed)

- Cold plants were deleted (not shed) on brownout, and a single plant always browned out — the first $25k plant vanished. Now shed plants wait offline and return; one plant covers its own demand.
- Auto-restock compared absolute unit cost to a $0.85 cap.
- Control accrued as 0.002% of cash per second, making lanes free for a rich player. Now log-scaled.
- Barges and franchises had flat prices (runaway). Now geometric.
- Chain Attention drain ran after the refill, so a full bar was impossible with stores; cap-sized memos were unbuyable. Tourney While Full also ate the bar a desk memo was waiting for.
- Reach-in memo only appeared after produce spoiled, which could stall the cold path for tidy players.
- Per-tick sale carry was capped at 2 units regardless of tick size.
